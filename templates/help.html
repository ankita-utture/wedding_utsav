Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about planning your wedding on Wedding Utsav.

How does the Smart Budget Planner work?

When you enter your total wedding budget, our system automatically splits the amount based on standard Indian wedding expense weights (50% Venue, 20% Catering, 15% DJ, 15% Parlour/Makeup). If you select only a few services, the app dynamically shifts and recalculates percentages so that your total budget is optimized across your selected choices.

How do I contact and book a vendor?

Browse the categories on the dashboard (Venues, DJs, Catering, Parlour). Apply filters to match your wedding date and budget. Once you find a suitable vendor, click on "Get Contact Details". The app will immediately display the vendor's direct phone number, email, and location so you can call them directly.

What does the "Double-Booking Prevention" feature do?

To prevent planning stress, when you search for vendors on a specific date, our system queries active bookings. If a vendor is already booked by another user on your chosen date, they will either be hidden or marked as "Booked/Unavailable" so you can choose an alternative vendor.

How can I list my services as a Vendor?

Log out and go to the registration page. Select "Vendor" in the role dropdown and fill in your business name, service category, base price, and description. Once submitted, our Admin will review and verify your account. Once verified, your services will automatically start appearing in customer budget searches.

Is there a fee to use Wedding Utsav?

Wedding Utsav is completely free for couples. We help you find local vendors directly with zero brokerage or middleman fees. For vendors, a flat monthly listing subscription model or low-commission models are available to get direct leads.
